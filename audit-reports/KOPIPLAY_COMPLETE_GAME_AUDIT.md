## Risk Classification Methodology

- **Category A (OFFICIAL DISTRIBUTION)**: Games integrated through identifiable, authorized distribution syndicates (GameDistribution / Azerion B.V., HTMLGames / Zygomatic B.V., GameMonetize official feeds). These providers explicitly license free ad-supported iframe embedding on web portals, while retaining advertising revenue and game copyright.
- **Category B (DIRECT DEVELOPER LICENSE)**: First-party proprietary games created specifically for KopiPlay. Full ownership resides with the project operator.
- **Category C (THIRD-PARTY PORTAL EMBED)**: Games embedded from open community distribution networks where the original studio copyright has not been independently confirmed.
- **Category D (DOWNLOADED GAME FILES)**: Locally hosted game directories stored within `c:/online game/games/`.
- **Category E (UNKNOWN SOURCE)**: Games where no original source or rights holder can be reliably established. (0 games in current catalog).
- **Category F (POSSIBLE COPYRIGHT / TRADEMARK CONCERN)**: Games utilizing gameplay or title names echoing established commercial trademarks (e.g., *Breakout*, *Arkanoid*, *Tetris*, *Pac-Man*).

## Legal & Commercial Deployment Guidance

### 1. GameDistribution (Azerion B.V., Netherlands) – 47 Games
- **Embedding Authorization:** GameDistribution grants webmasters non-exclusive rights to embed its HTML5 catalog via unique GUID endpoints. In-game video and interstitial ads are served by Azerion's ad exchange.
- **Commercial Restrictions:** Webmasters **cannot** sell, sublicense, or claim copyright ownership over any GameDistribution game files. 
- **Action Required for Domain Launch:** When a custom domain is purchased, the operator must register a free Publisher Account at [gamedistribution.com/publishers](https://gamedistribution.com/publishers) and register the live domain to receive revenue attribution.

### 2. HTMLGames (Zygomatic B.V., Netherlands) – 121 Games
- **Embedding Authorization:** Provided via the official webmaster syndication feed at [htmlgames.com/html5-games-for-your-site/](https://www.htmlgames.com/html5-games-for-your-site/).
- **Commercial Restrictions:** Free embeds must remain unmodified and display Zygomatic's in-game pre-roll advertising. Zygomatic's terms state: *"You are not allowed to sell, license, rent or otherwise transfer the games or feeds."* For white-label or unbranded commercial deployment, a paid commercial license must be purchased from `info@zygomatic.nl`.

### 3. First-Party Local Games (KopiPlay Studios & Arcade) – 10 Games
- **Ownership:** Fully proprietary code and procedural assets owned 100% by KopiPlay.
- **Status:** Safe for commercial website operation, domain deployment, and asset transfer.

### 4. GameMonetize (12 Games)
- **Status:** Operates as an open syndication network. 1 studio is verified (MAPI Games) and 1 identified publisher (Immanitas Entertainment). The remaining 10 titles are community uploads where the chain-of-title should be verified prior to commercial domain launch.
